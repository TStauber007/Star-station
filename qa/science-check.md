# Star Station: science check

Reviewer: independent science review (2026-10-17). I read only `docs/SCRIPT.md` and `docs/SCIENCE_SOURCES.md`, and spot-checked some sources with web searches. Opening pages directly was blocked here too (DNS failures), so each check relied on what the search results showed.

## Summary

I checked **83** narrator lines (or tight groups of lines) that say or imply something about real science or spaceflight. **78 are ACCURATE**, **2 NEED FIX** and **3 are NOT SOURCED**. The two fixes are small wording changes. M1 `p6` "The Earth was coming into view!" suggests the Earth comes and goes from the window, when from the station it is always right there below. M2 `p8` "reaching *up* toward the lights" goes against the script's own "no up or down in space" rule. The three not-sourced lines are probably true but have no entry in `SCIENCE_SOURCES.md`: hair floating out in every direction (M1 `p2`), the root drying out (M2 `p5-peek`), and the zinnias being orange (M2 `p1` card and `p8`). The orange colour checks out: the first Veggie zinnia, in January 2016, was orange. The science is careful overall. Floating is explained as falling around the Earth, not "no gravity", and the night, battery and Moon-phase lines are right. There are **no age-appropriateness flags**. Every mistake path (leaving Button behind, peeking at the seed, hiding the panel, squeezing in, hogging the telescope, hiding under the blanket) ends in a kind repair with no shaming. Five optional wording polishes, which are not counted as flags, are listed at the end.

## Needs fix

### 1. M1 `p6`: "The Earth was coming into view!"
- **Reason:** A 4-year-old will picture the Earth drifting into the window and away again. In reality the station's Earth-facing windows always have the Earth right there, filling the view below. What changes is day and night. The blue glow in the line already reads as sunlight coming back onto the Earth, so the fix is to say that.
- **Suggested replacement:** "The Sun was shining on the Earth below!" (keep "It's the best view on the whole station.")

### 2. M2 `p8`: "And look! Little green plants, reaching up toward the lights."
- **Reason:** The script already took out "Down, down, down" for roots and "floats up, up, up" for hair, because in orbit there's no up or down (M6 `p3` says so too). "Reaching *up*" brings back the Earth idea that plants grow upward. The very next line teaches the real point: they grow toward the light. Dropping one word fixes it.
- **Suggested replacement:** "And look! Little green plants, reaching toward the lights."

## Not sourced (probably true; add a source or soften)

1. **M1 `p2`, "Abigail's hair floats out in every direction."** True. Add a NASA or ESA photo or video of an astronaut's floating hair, for example NASA astronaut Karen Nyberg's 2013 hair-washing video from the ISS.
2. **M2 `p5-peek`, "the little root began to dry out."** Probably true, but there's no source. Either cite a gardening page on young roots drying when exposed, or soften it so it makes no science claim: *"But the little root didn't like being out of its cozy pillow."*
3. **M2 `p1` card and `p8`, "bright orange" zinnias.** True. The first zinnia that bloomed in Veggie (Scott Kelly, Jan 16, 2016) was orange ([EarthSky](https://earthsky.org/space/first-flower-blooms-in-space/), [Smithsonian](https://www.smithsonianmag.com/smart-news/check-out-first-flower-grown-space-180957859)). Add this to F2.7 and drop its "not checked" note.

## Age-appropriateness

No flags. The scary and sad moments are short and get fixed kindly right away. Button is left behind (M1), the peeked seed starts to dry out (M2), Button blames itself and its battery runs low on the hide path (M3), there's an "Ow" in the squeeze (M4), and there's the dark tunnel and the "GURGLE!" (M5). Wrong choices lead to gentle, natural results and then a repair ("Bumps happen. Let's fix it together!"), not to blame. One reading tip for the 2½-year-old: say the M5 `p8` "GURGLE!" in a silly voice rather than a loud one.

## Optional polish (not counted as flags)

- **M4 `p6`:** "Tap, and watch the Moon grow!" could be "Tap, and watch the Moon change shape!" Button corrects it on the next page, so this can stay if you like the set-up and pay-off.
- **M1 `p7-show`:** "floated down the station" could be "floated along the station", if you want to avoid every "down".
- **M5 `p9`:** "Where people are sleeping." could be "Where lots of people are sleeping." (Many people on the night side are still awake. That's partly why the lights are on.)
- **M2 `p2`:** Real Veggie seeds are packed into the pillows on Earth and woken up with water in orbit. Tucking seeds in is fine as a story action. Just don't add "real astronauts" to it.
